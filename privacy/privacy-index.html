Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Money IQ ("the App") is published by Wealth Ocean Institute, Mysuru, Karnataka, India. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to use the quiz features.

The only data points used are:

  • A display name you optionally enter for your score card and challenge link (stored only on your device).
  • Your quiz score and selected quiz mode — stored locally to display your result.
  • Standard device identifiers used by Google AdMob to show ads (see Section 3).

2. Photos and Camera

📷 Your photo is captured and processed entirely on your device. It is never uploaded to any server, never shared with third parties, and is deleted when you leave the result screen.

The App uses your camera only to generate the score-card image that you can optionally save to your gallery or share via messaging apps of your choice. This action is always initiated by you.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and use:

  • Advertising ID (GAID) — a resettable device identifier
  • Approximate location (country/region) derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completions)
  • Device information (OS version, screen size)

This data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalisation.

4. WhatsApp Contact

The App includes an optional button to contact a Wealth Ocean Institute financial expert via WhatsApp. Tapping this button opens WhatsApp on your device with a pre-filled message; no message is sent automatically. You choose whether to send it. We do not collect or store your WhatsApp number or any message content.

5. Challenge Links

When you share a challenge, the App encodes your display name, score, and quiz mode into a link. This information is included in the link URL and visible to anyone you share it with. It is not stored on any server operated by Wealth Ocean Institute.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Data Storage and Security

All quiz data (score, name, mode) is stored in your device's local storage only and is not transmitted to our servers. We do not operate any backend server that receives data from the App.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • WhatsApp — user-initiated contact (WhatsApp's own policy applies)

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
